# AI-Research-and-Engineering

**AI Research and Engineering** (An AI for All Initiative) is a public collection of code, documentation, experiments, and research in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning.

This repository documents my journey in learning, exploring, and engineering AI/ML solutions. Its purpose is to make knowledge and practical implementations accessible to everyone.

## Purpose and Scope

This repository covers a wide range of AI/ML topics, including:

- **Machine Learning**
  - Supervised Learning
  - Unsupervised Learning
  - Semi-Supervised Learning
  - Self-Supervised Learning
  - Reinforcement Learning
  - Model Evaluation and Feature Engineering

- **Deep Learning**
  - Artificial Neural Networks
  - Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs)
  - Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs)
  - Transformers and Attention Mechanisms
  - Autoencoders and Generative Models
  - Optimization and Backpropagation

- **Agentic AI**
  - AI Agents and Tool Calling
  - Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)
  - Multi-Agent Systems
  - Agent Orchestration and Workflows
  - Model Context Protocol (MCP)
  - Local and Open-Source LLMs

- **AI Mathematics and Fundamentals**
  - Linear Algebra
  - Calculus and Optimization
  - Probability and Statistics
  - Mathematical Foundations of Machine Learning

- **Data Science and Analytics**
  - Exploratory Data Analysis
  - Data Preprocessing and Feature Engineering
  - Statistical Modeling
  - Time-Series Analysis
  - Anomaly Detection
  - Principal Component Analysis

- **Computer Vision**
  - Image Classification
  - Object Detection
  - Image Segmentation
  - Image Processing

- **Applied AI Research and Engineering**
  - Research Experiments and Reproductions
  - Predictive Modeling
  - Intelligent Automation
  - AI Applications in Energy and Power Systems
  - Other Practical AI/ML Projects

## Who Is This For?

Whether you're a beginner learning AI/ML fundamentals, a student exploring research, or a practitioner experimenting with new techniques, you're welcome to explore the code, study the implementations, and use the resources to support your own learning journey.

Most projects reflect my ongoing learning and experimentation. Larger and more mature projects will eventually have their own dedicated repositories.

**Learn. Explore. Experiment. Build. — AI for All.**
